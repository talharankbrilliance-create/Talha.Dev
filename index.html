Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what clients usually ask before we start a project.

What platforms do you work with?
↗

Mainly WordPress with Elementor and Divi, and Shopify for e‑commerce storefronts. I also write custom code where a page builder alone isn't enough.

How long does a typical website take?
↗

A standard business site usually takes 1–2 weeks depending on the number of pages and revisions. E‑commerce stores can take a bit longer depending on catalog size.

Do you offer revisions?
↗

Yes — every project includes revision rounds until the design and functionality match what we agreed on before starting.

Can you maintain a site after launch?
↗

Yes, I offer ongoing maintenance plugin/theme updates, bug fixes, speed checks, and small content changes on a monthly or as-needed basis.

Will my site be mobile responsive and fast?
↗

Every site I build is tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop, with image optimization and caching so pages load quickly on all devices.

Do you offer page speed optimization for existing sites?
↗

Yes, I audit your Core Web Vitals, compress and lazy-load images, set up caching, minify code, and clean up bloated plugins to bring load times down without changing your design.

How do we get started?
↗

Reach out via email or phone with a short brief of what you need. I'll follow up with questions, a timeline, and next steps.
